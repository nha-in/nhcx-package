# T21. PMJAY Discharge Stage Before the Final Claim

#### T21D. DESCRIPTION

PMJAY validates the discharge before it takes the final claim [PAYER](../references/PAYERS.md#markers): the first send from the Claim tab is the discharge, on `v1/claim/submit` with `Claim.use` `claim` under workflow 14, carrying the discharge on `supportingInfo` and the discharge summary under `HDS` ([A5. Claim Submit](../apis/A5-claim-submit.md), [D20. claim_submission](../database/D20-claim-submission.md)). The payer answers on `claim/on_submit` ([C6. Claim Reply](../callbacks/C6-claim-on-submit.md)): 261 approved, 262 rejected, 263 queried. A queried discharge is answered on 141; a rejected one is corrected and sent again on 14, never reprocessed; only an approved discharge opens the final claim on 15. The discharge's verdict is kept beside the final claim, and its approval is never read as the claim's.

#### T21S. SETUP

Two PMJAY claims with an approved pre-authorisation (T14. PMJAY Pre-authorisation Through the Payer Service (in nhcx-preauth)), the IPD admission linked on the Claim tab, a discharge recorded and the discharge summary filed under `HDS` ([S11. Claim Submission](../screens/S11-claim-submission.md)), each with the Discharge Consent answered on the claim's forms card unless a stage-Discharge token is held (T19. PMJAY Beneficiary Verification and ABHA (in nhcx-coverage)). The integration decision `claim.dischargeStep` is `where-payer-requires` (L1.A). The desk steps follow [T17. PMJAY Claim Through the Role Walk](T17-pmjay-claim-adjudicated.md): the NHCX Payer Service ([A14. Adjudicator User Role](../apis/A14-adjudicator-user-role.md), [A15. Adjudicator Process Case](../apis/A15-adjudicator-process-case.md)) decides the discharge as it decides a claim; when it does not hold the discharge, the step is recorded `blocked` with the desk's answer, never skipped as passed.

#### T21G. GUI

1. First claim, [S11. Claim Submission](../screens/S11-claim-submission.md): the card reads "Discharge" with the "Discharge validation first" alert; press "Submit discharge" and confirm; payer side: `query` cycle with a remark; answer on [S11. Claim Submission](../screens/S11-claim-submission.md) with "Answer the discharge query", attaching one new document; payer side: `approve` cycle; wait for "Discharge approved"; press "Submit claim" for the final claim; payer side: `approve` cycle; wait for the decision.
2. Second claim, [S11. Claim Submission](../screens/S11-claim-submission.md): "Submit discharge"; payer side: `reject` cycle; the card reads "Discharge rejected" with no Reprocess section; correct the discharge and press "Submit discharge" again.

#### T21X. EXPECT

- The first send of each claim goes on `v1/claim/submit` with `x-hcx-workflow_id` 14, `x-hcx-status` `request.initiated`, `x-hcx-use_case` `New`, `Claim.use` `claim`, the discharge on `supportingInfo` and the discharge summary under `HDS`; the flash reads "Discharge submitted to the payer."; [D9. claim](../database/D9-claim.md) reads stage `discharge`, sub-stage `requested`; [D20. claim_submission](../database/D20-claim-submission.md) `submission_kind` `discharge`, `discharge_status` `submitting`, `discharge_correlation_id` the send's correlation id.
- While the discharge is with the payer, another send is refused with "The claim is with the payer; wait for its answer before sending again."
- The discharge query (263, or a queried ClaimResponse) leaves the leg and `discharge_status` `queried`, the chip "Discharge queried" and the S6 next action "Answer the discharge query"; the answer goes under 141 with `Resubmit`, the Claim anchored `claim/queryupdate`.
- The discharge approval (261, or an approving ClaimResponse) leaves `discharge_status` `approved`, the chip "Discharge approved", the S6 next action "File the final claim", and S12's Approved figure `-`: a discharge approval is not the claim's.
- The final claim goes under 15 with `New`; [D20. claim_submission](../database/D20-claim-submission.md) then reads `submission_kind` `claim` with `discharge_status` still `approved`, [D9. claim](../database/D9-claim.md) stage `claim`; once the claim is decided, another send is refused with "The payer has decided this claim; it goes back for another look as a reprocess request, not as a claim sent again."
- The rejected discharge (262) reads "Discharge rejected" with the next action "Correct and resubmit the discharge"; a reprocess is refused with "Only a claim the payer has rejected can be sent back for reprocessing." and a balance release with "Only a decided claim can have its balance asked for."; the discharge goes again under 14.
- A refusal at the door on a discharge send leaves `discharge_status` `error` and the next send a discharge again; a late reply on the discharge's correlation id after the final claim went is `ignored` by [C6. Claim Reply](../callbacks/C6-claim-on-submit.md).
- Each run is recorded in `e2e.json` as for [T17. PMJAY Claim Through the Role Walk](T17-pmjay-claim-adjudicated.md), with the workflow id of every send.
