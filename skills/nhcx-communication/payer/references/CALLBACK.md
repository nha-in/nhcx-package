# Callbacks

Every message NHCX delivers to the application, in one list. Each row links to its full spec in [../callbacks/](../callbacks/INDEX.md). NHCX posts to the application's public route (`/in/<path>` or `/v1/<path>`); [G8. Receive](../gateway/G8-receive.md) decrypts it and calls C1 in-process, then records the message and C1's outcome in [G9. Ledger](../gateway/G9-ledger.md). C1 routes by the bundle's focal resource, not by the path. Every handler answers `settled`, `unmatched`, `ignored`, `rejected` or `error`; only `error` makes NHCX redeliver.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [C1](../callbacks/C1-callback-door.md) | Callback Door | every route | Takes each decrypted message, classifies it by its bundle (not the path), drops a redelivery by `x-hcx-api_call_id`, requires sender, recipient and correlation id, archives it, and routes it to C2 to C11. | the bundle's focal resource | [D1](../database/D1-payer.md), [D3](../database/D3-document-type.md), [D19](../database/D19-case.md), [D27](../database/D27-case-exchange-message.md), [D28](../database/D28-nhcx-delivery.md), [D31](../database/D31-audit-log.md) | [A5](../apis/A5-query-request.md) | none |
